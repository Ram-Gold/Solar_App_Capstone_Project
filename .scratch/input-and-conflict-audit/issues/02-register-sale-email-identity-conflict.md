# Phone-only customer lookup creates duplicate emails or silently overwrites confirmed customer emails

## Problem & Verification
Verified in `RegisterSaleScreen.jsx`, `customerService.js` (`registerCustomerAndSale`), and live Supabase `customers` constraints (`customers_phone_number_key` is UNIQUE on `phone_number`; `email` has no unique constraint):

1. In `RegisterSaleScreen`, returning customer lookup queries only `searchCustomerByPhone(phoneNumber)`.
2. If an existing customer uses a new phone number with their existing email, `registerCustomerAndSale` creates a **second** `customers` row sharing that email while `provisionCustomerAuthAccount` fails/logs a warning because the email already exists in `auth.users`, fragmenting the customer's warranty records across two QR tokens.
3. Conversely, if staff enters an existing customer's `phone_number` paired with a *different* email address, `registerCustomerAndSale` overwrites `customers.email` and resets `email_confirmed_at = null`, disconnecting the customer's verified portal login without an explicit confirmation prompt.

## Recommended Triage
- **Category:** `bug`
- **Recommended State:** `ready-for-agent` (surface duplicate-email match warnings during POS phone/email entry and require explicit staff confirmation before overwriting a confirmed email or linking a second phone).

## Agent Brief

**Category:** bug
**Summary:** Detect duplicate-email customer records and confirm before overwriting confirmed emails in in-store sales registration

**Current behavior:**
Customer resolution in the point-of-sale flow (`registerCustomerAndSale` and `RegisterSaleScreen`) searches exclusively by normalized `phone_number` via `searchCustomerByPhone()`. When an email already exists under another phone number, a duplicate `customers` row is inserted with a conflicting email while auth account creation fails. When a returning phone number is submitted with a new email, `customers.email` is silently overwritten and `email_confirmed_at` is reset to `null`.

**Desired behavior:**
Before inserting a new customer or updating an existing record's email:
1. Check whether the normalized `email` already belongs to another `customers` record (`phone_number !== normalizedPhone`). If found, surface the existing customer record (name, masked/display phone, email) in the POS UI and allow staff either to load that existing customer profile (adding the purchase to their existing QR portal token) or explicitly confirm creating a separate record / updating contact info.
2. When an existing phone-matched customer already has a confirmed email (`email_confirmed_at` non-null) and the submitted email differs, require an explicit confirmation step in the POS UI before overwriting `customers.email` and invalidating `email_confirmed_at`.
3. Enforce clean email-normalization (`sanitizeEmail`) on both lookup and persistence.

**Key interfaces:**
- `searchCustomerByPhone(phoneNumber)` and a companion `searchCustomerByEmail(email)` helper in the customer service module returning `{ customer, error }`
- `registerCustomerAndSale({ phoneNumber, fullName, email, password, branchLocation, items, ticketId, onEmailStatusChange, allowEmailOverwrite })`: rejects or requires explicit opt-in if overwriting a confirmed email or colliding with another customer's email unless confirmed
- `RegisterSaleScreen` UI state displaying conflict warnings and one-click loading of an email-matched returning customer

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] Entering an email already belonging to another customer displays a visible conflict warning with an option to load the existing customer profile
- [ ] Submitting a new email for a phone-matched customer who already has a confirmed email requires explicit staff confirmation before updating the email
- [ ] No duplicate customer auth account error occurs when attaching a purchase to an email-matched returning customer
- [ ] Unit/integration tests cover both email collision and confirmed-email overwrite confirmation paths

**Out of scope:**
- Merging historical duplicate customer rows in the database
- Changing the uniqueness constraint on `customers.phone_number`
