  • Separate Two-Door Landing Screen: Users choose Help Me Choose (guided steps) or I Know My Specs (direct numeric form) upfront, with a one-click switch at any time.
  • Zero Sliders (RAM-12): All numeric fields use inputmode="numeric" paired with one-tap preset pills.
  • Verified Static Metadata Map: Uses a deterministic lookup map by product.type (covering the 12 Solar types) for Form Factor (All-in-One vs. Split-Type) and Mounting Style—zero DB changes, zero regex guessing.
  • Mixed Results with Honest Badges: Priced streetlights and NULL-price floodlights appear in one ranked list; unpriced floodlights receive a neutral 70% budget score and a clear Price on Inquiry badge.
  • Per-Unit / Total Budget + Branch Stock Check: Quantity multiplies unit price ranges and checks against live Quiapo + Cavite branch stock; Need a Pole acts as a ranking preference + quote note.
  • Solar-Focused Scope: Scoped to the 51 Solar products, with a clear link to browse the 50 AC Grid (220V) products.
